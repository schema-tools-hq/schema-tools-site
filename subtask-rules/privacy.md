# Privacy Policy — Subtask Rules

**Effective date: 4 August 2026 · Vendor: Schema Tools**

## Summary

Subtask Rules runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, inside Atlassian's
infrastructure. **We do not operate any servers, and your data never leaves
Atlassian.**

## What the app accesses

The app reads, with the acting user's own permissions: the project's issue
types, project details (to detect team-managed projects) and workflow
configuration. When a Jira administrator enables enforcement, the app updates
the project's workflow to attach its validator.

## What the app stores

Your rules configuration (a matrix of issue type IDs and an enabled flag) is
stored as a project property inside your own Jira site. Nothing is stored
outside Atlassian, and nothing is sent to the vendor or any third party.

## Personal data

None. The app does not collect, store or process any personal data — the
stored configuration contains only Jira issue type IDs and a true/false flag.

## Egress

None. The app declares no external endpoints and is eligible for Atlassian's
"Runs on Atlassian" program.
