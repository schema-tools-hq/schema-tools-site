# Privacy Policy — Sprint Scope Change & Scope Creep

**Effective date: 1 August 2026 · Vendor: Schema Tools**

## Summary

Sprint Scope Change & Scope Creep runs entirely on Atlassian Forge, inside
Atlassian's infrastructure. **We do not operate any servers, and your data
never leaves Atlassian.**

## What the app accesses

The app reads, with the viewing user's own permissions: Jira boards, sprints,
issues (summary, status, estimate, created date) and issue change histories of
the Sprint field. It does not modify any Jira data.

## What the app stores

Computed per-sprint summaries for closed sprints (issue keys, category flags,
timestamps, change authors, totals) are cached in Forge app storage, hosted by
Atlassian, to speed up repeat views. No data is sent to the vendor or any
third party. Uninstalling the app removes its storage per Atlassian's Forge
data lifecycle.

## Personal data

The app displays the names of users who changed sprint scope, as recorded by
Jira in issue histories. This data stays inside your Atlassian site. The
vendor has no access to it.

## Egress

None. The app declares no external endpoints and is eligible for Atlassian's
"Runs on Atlassian" program.
